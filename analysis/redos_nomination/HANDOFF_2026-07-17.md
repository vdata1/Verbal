## What this work is

`nominate_probe.py` flags ReDoS-prone regexes *without running a big string*: take a
chaos mutant (non-matching → forces full backtracking), build a middle-deletion length
**ladder**, sweep it from tiny n, fit `log t ~ n` (exp) vs `log t ~ log n` (poly),
classify. Everything stays in the cheap regime because 2ⁿ explodes fast. Prior state and
the method rationale are in `NOTES.md`; this note covers only what changed today.

## The three fixes (all landed)

| # | Failure | Fix | File |
|---|---------|-----|------|
| 1 | 6580, 9577 → UNCLASSIFIED | poly-degree disambiguation in `classify()` | `nominate_probe.py` |
| 2 | 2 false EXPONENTIALs | absolute cost floor, **EXPONENTIAL verdict only** | `nominate_probe.py` |
| 3 | 7984 → ERROR (harness hang) | per-rung worker-thread watchdog → HANG verdict | `ladder_harness.js` |

- **Fix 1.** The handoff's proposed "knee / longest-clean-exponential-prefix" idea was
  tried and **rejected against the real curves** — 9577's ladder is non-monotone (len-34
  dips below len-33), so a clean-prefix scan finds 0 points. The discriminant that works
  is the **fitted polynomial degree**: true exponentials force k>8 (6580:10, 9577:17,
  the new 8478:20, controls:8–11); real polynomials sit at k≈2. `classify()` now calls
  EXPONENTIAL when the exp fit is decent (R²≥`EXP_R2_MIN`, base≥1.1) AND the competing
  poly needs an absurd degree (k>`POLY_K_MAX`). Real polys (k≤6) never trip it.
- **Fix 2.** Both false positives peaked at `dearest 0.001ms` (timer noise). Floor is
  `ABS_FLOOR_MS`=1ms on the dearest rung, **scoped to EXPONENTIAL**. NOT global: all 49
  POLYNOMIAL nominations peak sub-10µs, so a global floor deletes the whole bucket.
- **Fix 3.** `re.test()` is uninterruptible in-thread → timing runs in a worker, main
  thread watchdogs it; a rung past `PER_RUNG_MS`=1s → `worker.terminate()` (verified to
  interrupt a live native regex on V8/node 26) → `{hung, hung_len}` → HANG verdict.
  node/bun bounded; deno keeps the legacy inline path (not in the corpus run).

## The one open question (deliberately left for you)

Every POLYNOMIAL/UNCLASSIFIED nomination peaks **sub-10µs** (median 0.000ms, max 0.010ms).
At nominate-time these can't be told apart from fit-noise — a real cheap O(n²) at 40 chars
is *also* sub-10µs. `regex_8848`-as-POLYNOMIAL (k=1.87 on 3 sub-µs points) is representative,
not an outlier. **This is a confirm-phase question (run a longer input), not a classify
one.** Decisions waiting on you:
1. Do POLYNOMIAL/UNCLASSIFIED get their own (lower) floor, or feed straight to a confirm
   pass that runs one long input per nominee? The 49+140 = 189 candidates are small enough
   to confirm serially.
2. `EXP_R2_MIN`=0.95 has only ~0.015 margin on the two real cases (0.964, 0.967). A future
   wobblier exponential could fall to UNCLASSIFIED (still nominated, just mislabeled).
   Watch for this if you widen the corpus.
